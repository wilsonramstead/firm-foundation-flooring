Why homeowners call Firm Foundation

A floor is only as good as what's under it.

We don't just lay planks — we level the subfloor, respect your deadline, and leave a finish that looks right from every angle. That's why Pinellas County neighbors keep recommending us.

Uneven slab? We level the floor first — even after concrete work or plumbing repairs — so your new plank or tile sits flat and lasts.
